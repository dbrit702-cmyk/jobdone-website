Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

JobDone ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the JobDone mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password
  • Business information: business name, phone number, address, and ABN/VAT number
  • Invoice data: client names, email addresses, line items, and payment information
  • Voice recordings: audio captured when you use the voice invoice feature (processed and not stored)
  • Logo images: business logos you upload to appear on invoices

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the JobDone app
  • Process and send invoices and quotes on your behalf
  • Transcribe voice recordings into invoice data
  • Send automated payment reminders to your clients
  • Send you app updates and support communications
  • Process subscription payments

Voice Data

When you use the voice invoice feature, your audio is transmitted to our secure transcription service for processing. Audio recordings are not permanently stored — they are processed in real time and immediately discarded after transcription. The resulting text transcript may be stored as part of your invoice record.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your invoice and business data is stored in the United States.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the app, including:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Cloudflare — invoice delivery and voice processing
  • Email delivery services — sending invoices to your clients

Microphone Access

JobDone requests access to your device's microphone solely for the purpose of recording voice invoices. Microphone access is only active when you explicitly tap the record button. We do not access your microphone at any other time.

Photo Library Access

JobDone requests access to your photo library solely for uploading your business logo. We do not access, store, or share any other photos from your device.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your invoice data at any time via the CSV export feature

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and invoice records will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

JobDone is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by email or through the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at support@getjobdoneinvoice.app
