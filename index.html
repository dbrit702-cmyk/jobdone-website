How it works

Three steps.
Get paid.

01

Speak your invoice

Just say the client name, what you did, and the amount. JobDone transcribes your voice and builds the invoice automatically.

02

Review and adjust

Check the details, edit any line items, add GST or notes. Everything is editable before you send.

03

Send and get paid

Hit send. A professional PDF invoice lands in your client's inbox instantly. Track who's paid, who hasn't, and send reminders automatically.
