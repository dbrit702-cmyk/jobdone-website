Common Questions

How does the voice invoice feature work? +
Tap the mic button on the home screen and speak naturally — say the client name, what work you did, and the amounts. JobDone transcribes your voice and automatically builds the invoice. You can then review and edit everything before sending.
What does the client receive? +
Your client receives a professional PDF invoice via email. It includes your business name, logo, contact details, a full list of line items, totals, GST (if applicable), and payment due date.
How do automatic payment reminders work? +
When reminders are enabled in Settings, JobDone automatically sends follow-up emails to clients with unpaid invoices at 7, 14, and 30 days. You can turn reminders on or off at any time.
Can I send quotes as well as invoices? +
Yes — just say "quote for..." instead of "invoice for..." when recording. You can also toggle between invoice and quote in the manual entry screen. Quotes can be converted to invoices with a single tap once the job is done.
How do I cancel my subscription? +
You can cancel anytime from your device's subscription settings (App Store or Google Play). Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your invoice data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. Voice recordings are processed in real time and never permanently stored.
Can I export my invoices? +
Yes — go to the Invoices tab and tap "Export CSV" to download all your invoice data as a spreadsheet. Great for tax time or sharing with your accountant.
How do I delete my account? +
Email us at support@getjobdoneinvoice.app and we'll permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
